# Hannah — Sensory Management & Energy Conservation

*Context:* Hannah has LC + ADHD. ADHD weakens sensory gating — more input gets through to conscious processing. Combined with LC mitochondrial dysfunction, sensory overload is a real metabolic cost on top of physical exertion. Leaving the house is a guaranteed crash because it stacks physical, sensory, social, and executive load simultaneously.

Goal: reduce sensory processing tax at home to conserve energy budget for healing.

---

## Auditory
- ✅ Earphones/noise canceling already in use — biggest single win
- Brown or pink noise preferred over music (lyrics = cognitive load)
- Silence > background TV (TV is constant unpredictable sensory demand)

## Visual
- Blue light filtering glasses to reduce visual processing fatigue
- Dim, warm lighting — bright overhead lights are high stimulus
- Reduce visual clutter in immediate environment
- Matte screens over glossy (less glare processing)
- Dark mode on all devices

## Smell
- Unscented everything — cleaning products, candles, visitors' perfume
- Scent is processed by the oldest brain structures, very direct, hard to suppress
- One of the most overlooked sensory drains

## Touch / Proprioception
- Soft, seamless clothing — sensory-friendly fabrics
- Weighted blanket — calming via predictable proprioceptive input
- Temperature control — thermal dysregulation common in dysautonomia

## Social / Cognitive
- Text over voice calls when possible — async, lower bandwidth demand
- Single-tasking strictly — no background media while working
- Time-box cognitive tasks with full rest periods between

---

## The Principle
Every sensory channel she quiets = metabolic budget returned for repair.
She's not being precious — she's conserving resources her mitochondria need to heal.

## Leaving the House
Leaving the house stacks simultaneously:
- Physical exertion (walking, standing)
- Full sensory firehose (sights, sounds, smells, touch)
- Social processing (reading faces, responding, masking)
- Executive function (navigation, scheduling, decisions)
- Anxiety overhead of being "out"

For Hannah's phenotype this is 5–10 activities happening at once against a depleted energy budget. Guaranteed crash until baseline is established and capacity is mapped.

## Remote/Async Work Implication
TA role should be fully remote and async for the foreseeable future. Not just convenient — medically appropriate for her specific ADHD + LC phenotype.
