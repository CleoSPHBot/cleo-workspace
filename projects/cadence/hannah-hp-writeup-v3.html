Assessment: This patient’s presentation is most consistent with Long COVID with ME/CFS phenotype, characterized by: (1) post-infectious onset following SARS-CoV-2 + EBV co-infection, (2) severe PEM with 3-day lag demonstrated biometrically, (3) multisystem involvement, (4) autonomic dysregulation, and (5) near-complete failure to recover over 4 years. The leading mechanistic hypothesis, supported by emerging literature (Freire 2026, Iwasaki Lab), is persistent viral antigen/spike protein burden in gut and other tissues, driving ongoing immune dysregulation, HPA axis disruption, and microbiome perturbation. Alternative or co-contributing diagnoses include: - Persistent EBV reactivation: VCA IgM was reactive June 2025, negative June 2026 — acute phase resolved; latent EBV with potential immunomodulatory effect persists. Valacyclovir protocol (Yale/Iwasaki) remains a pending discussion pending patient-submitted evidence. - ME/CFS (independent of COVID): The severity and biometric pattern (PEM, 3-day lag, Ghali 2023 pacing adherence data) fulfills ME/CFS criteria per ICC/CCC. GET is contraindicated. - Small fiber neuropathy: Less likely given normal formal ART, but patchy QSART forearm is of uncertain significance. - Mast cell activation syndrome (MCAS): Tryptase 3.0 makes systemic mastocytosis unlikely; MCAS (without elevated tryptase) remains possible and may account for urticaria + multisystem symptoms. DO NOT MISS.

Plan: - Continue pacing protocol (WHOOP-guided Repair Spectrum Framework).
