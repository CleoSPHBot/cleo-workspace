## Dr. Tandon's Assessment

*"28 yo lady with multiple constellation of symptoms with main symptom being fatigue, malaise, with concern for if these are due to tick borne illness."*

**Key interpretations:**
- Lyme: **Negative** → post-Lyme syndrome unlikely (would expect IgG+)
- Babesia duncani IgG+/IgM−: Indicates past exposure, not active infection. Endemic on West Coast. Does not require chronic antibiotics.
- Anaplasma IgM+: Possible past illness; most healthy people recover spontaneously. Does not cause chronic infections requiring long-term antibiotics.
- Inflammatory markers normal, CBC normal, LFTs normal

**Differential diagnosis:**
- Infectious etiology unlikely
- Non-infectious causes to consider: thyroid, psychiatric issues, underlying lymphoma, rheumatologic disease, connective tissue disorder

**Recommendations:**
1. Check: HIV Ag/Ab, Babesia PCR, blood parasite smear, Anaplasma PCR, TPPA (syphilis), inflammatory markers, CBC, CMP
2. ⚠️ **Recommended stopping antibiotics** — no clear diagnosis of active infection; IDSA guidelines do not endorse chronic antibiotics for tick-borne illness; antibiotics have significant side effects (hepatotoxicity from rifampin)
3. Work up other etiologies: rheumatologic disorder, lymphoma, psychiatry follow-up
4. Sleep hygiene, diet, exercise, stress reduction, meditation

**Visit diagnoses:**

- Malaise
- Arthralgia of both knees
