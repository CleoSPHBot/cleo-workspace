# LC Phenotype Intake Questionnaire

**Purpose:** Classify new Cadence patients into Long COVID subgroups at onboarding.  
**Target:** ~10-15 questions, <5 minutes, mobile-friendly.  
**Output:** Phenotype placement on PEM/Dysautonomia · Cognitive/Neurological · Respiratory · Gut/Viral · Immune/Inflammatory axes.

---

## Section 1 — Background

**Q1. How long ago did you have COVID?**
- Less than 3 months
- 3–6 months
- 6–12 months
- More than 1 year

**Q2. How severe was your acute illness?**
- Mild (no medical care)
- Moderate (doctor visit, but not hospitalized)
- Severe (hospitalized)
- Unknown / asymptomatic

**Q3. Overall, how is your condition trending?**
- Slowly improving
- Stable (good days and bad days, but no clear trend)
- Slowly worsening
- Fluctuating unpredictably

---

## Section 2 — Post-Exertional Malaise (PEM)

**Q4. Do your symptoms get significantly worse after physical or mental effort?**
- No
- Yes, and it happens within a few hours
- Yes, and it hits 12–48 hours later
- Yes, but I can't predict when

**Q5. Have you had to give up activities you used to tolerate easily because they cause crashes?**
- No
- Sometimes
- Yes, regularly

*→ PEM/Dysautonomia signal: Q4 with 12-48h lag + Q5 yes*

---

## Section 3 — Autonomic / Dysautonomia

**Q6. Do you feel worse when standing up — lightheadedness, racing heart, or brain fog that improves when you sit or lie down?**
- No
- Occasionally
- Yes, most days

**Q7. Does heat make your symptoms significantly worse? (hot shower, warm weather)**
- No
- Somewhat
- Yes, noticeably

*→ Dysautonomia signal: Q6 yes + Q7 yes*

---

## Section 4 — Cognitive / Neurological

**Q8. How much does brain fog limit you, even on days when your fatigue is manageable?**
- Not at all
- Mildly — I notice it but can push through
- Moderately — it affects my work or daily tasks
- Severely — it's often my worst symptom

**Q9. Do you have new or worsened headaches, sensory sensitivities (light, sound, smell), or word-finding difficulties?**
- None of these
- One of these
- Two or more of these

*→ Cognitive/Neuro signal: Q8 moderate/severe + Q9 two or more*

---

## Section 5 — Gut / Viral Persistence

**Q10. Do you have GI symptoms (bloating, nausea, diarrhea, food sensitivities) that started or worsened after COVID?**
- No
- Mild / occasional
- Yes, significant

**Q11. Do you have clusters of bad days that feel almost like you're getting sick again — without a clear exertion trigger?**
- No
- Occasionally
- Yes, this is a pattern

*→ Gut/Viral signal: Q10 yes + Q11 yes*

---

## Section 6 — Respiratory

**Q12. Do you experience breathlessness or chest tightness at rest or with minimal activity?**
- No
- Occasionally
- Yes, regularly

*→ Respiratory signal: Q12 regularly*

---

## Section 7 — Immune / Inflammatory

**Q13. Since COVID, have you developed new skin rashes, joint pain, swollen lymph nodes, or allergic reactions to things you previously tolerated?**
- None
- One of these
- Two or more of these

*→ Immune/MCAS signal: Q13 two or more*

---

## Phenotype Scoring (Internal)

| Subgroup | Primary indicators |
|---|---|
| PEM/Dysautonomia | Q4 (12-48h lag), Q5 yes, Q6 yes, Q7 yes |
| Cognitive/Neurological | Q8 moderate+, Q9 2+ symptoms |
| Gut/Viral Persistence | Q10 significant, Q11 yes |
| Respiratory | Q12 regularly |
| Immune/Inflammatory | Q13 2+ symptoms |

A patient scores into a subgroup if they hit the primary indicators. Multiple subgroups = hybrid phenotype (e.g., Hannah = PEM/Dysautonomia + Gut/Viral).

---

## Notes

- Based on: DePaul Symptom Questionnaire (DSQ-2), COMPASS-31, published LC phenotyping literature
- Not a diagnostic instrument — used for Cadence personalization only
- Should be revisited every 3–6 months (phenotype can shift)
- Designed for mobile: single-choice per question, no free text required at intake
