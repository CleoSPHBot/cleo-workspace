# Hannah's Recovery Rules
*Simple, data-driven. Based on 774 days of WHOOP + Visible data.*

---

## The One Thing to Understand First

Your body has a 3-day lag. How you feel today is largely set by what you did 3 days ago — not yesterday. This means crashes feel random but they aren't. And it means the good days are *investments*, not rewards to spend.

---

## The Rules

### 🔴 Red Day (WHOOP recovery < 34)
**Full rest. No negotiation.**
- No exercise, no errands, no "just a quick thing"
- Horizontal rest counts — couch, bed, whatever works
- Cognitive rest too: limit screens, intense reading, stressful conversations
- *Why:* Red means your immune system is activated and your autonomic system is depleted. Any spend now extends the crash by days.

---

### 🟡 Yellow Day (WHOOP recovery 34–66)
**Gentle only. Stay well below your limit.**
- Light walking OK (< 20 min, HR stays low)
- PacePoints target: ≤ 8
- No social commitments that can't be cancelled
- *Why:* Yellow days feel like permission. They're not. This is the most dangerous zone — you feel well enough to spend but your reserves aren't rebuilt yet.

---

### 🟢 First Green Day after a bad stretch (recovery ≥ 67, first one after 3+ bad days)
**Rest day. Even though you feel good.**
- This is the hardest rule and the most important one
- Treat it like a yellow day — gentle only, PacePoints ≤ 8
- Do one enjoyable, low-effort thing. That's it.
- *Why:* The data shows that spending the first green day leads to crashing again within ~10 days. Resting through it leads to ~29 days of stability. The good feeling is your reserves refilling — don't drain them the moment they arrive.

---

### 🟢🟢 Second Consecutive Green Day
**Light activity OK. One thing.**
- One moderate activity (a walk, a short outing, focused work for 1–2 hours)
- PacePoints target: ≤ 12
- Rest after. Don't stack activities.
- *Why:* Two consecutive green days is when repair actually begins. The goal is to make it to day 3, not to catch up on everything from the bad week.

---

### 🟢🟢🟢 Third Green Day and Beyond
**You've earned a functional day.**
- Normal light activity is OK
- PacePoints target: ≤ 14 (your budget ceiling)
- Still avoid strain spikes ≥ 6 — one high-effort day resets the clock
- Check in with how you actually feel, not how you think you should feel
- *Why:* The Dec 14–19 run (your best stretch in recent data) stayed at 4.2–4.4 strain throughout. Low and steady is what held it.

---

### ⚠️ Lag-3 Watch
**If PacePoints were high 3 days ago, treat today like yellow — regardless of WHOOP.**
- If 3 days ago was > 14 PacePoints: plan for a quiet day today
- This is the strongest predictor in your data (correlation −0.74)
- The app will flag this automatically

---

## The Sleep Anchor

Everything runs through sleep. Before any other rule applies:

- Sleep performance < 60%: treat the day as yellow regardless of recovery score
- Sleep performance < 40%: treat as red
- Two consecutive bad sleep nights: mandatory rest day, no exceptions

Sleep is where HRV restores, cytokines clear, and the autonomic system resets. If sleep is broken, the other rules don't hold.

---

## The Big Picture

You're not trying to have one good day. You're trying to string enough good days together that your system can actually repair. The research says it takes 2+ consecutive green days before repair begins in earnest. The data says your loop runs quiet for ~20 days after a sustained green stretch — and up to 111 days when multiple green runs stack on top of each other (that was your Nov–Jan window).

The goal isn't to feel better tomorrow. It's to make the bad stretches shorter and less frequent over months.

**The three levers, in order:**
1. **Sleep** — protect it above everything else
2. **Pacing on good days** — especially the first green day
3. **Gut health** — SIM01, consistently, over months

None of these work alone. They have to work together long enough for the loop to downshift.
