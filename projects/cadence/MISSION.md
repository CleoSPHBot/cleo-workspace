## Clinical Foundation

The RECOVER-AUTONOMIC trial (ACC 2026, n=181) proved that coordinated care + lifestyle support significantly improves Long COVID POTS symptoms (p=0.0042) — while medication alone did not.

Cadence *is* that coordinated care layer, built for digital scale.

**Source:** [RECOVER-AUTONOMIC 2026](wiki/sources/recover-autonomic-2026.md)

## Who We're For

Patients with Long COVID — especially those with PEM, dysautonomia (POTS), and MCAS — who need more than a prescription. They need someone in their corner, every day.
