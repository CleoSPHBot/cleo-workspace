# Hannah Cognitive Rehabilitation Protocol
*Drafted: 2026-07-27 | Based on: Uswatte 2025 (CICT) + Vanova 2026 (UCL goal-oriented CR)*

---

## What This Is

A personalized, pacing-integrated cognitive rehabilitation program adapted from two recent RCTs showing improvements in Long COVID brain fog. Unlike those trials, this version is built around your energy budget — cognitive work counts as strain and lives inside the Repair Spectrum pacing system.

**Goal:** Gradually rebuild cognitive stamina and functional independence in a way that doesn't trigger PEM or set back your recovery arc.

---

## The Evidence Behind It

| Study | What They Did | Key Result |
|---|---|---|
| Uswatte 2025 (UAB) | 36h in-person: computerized brain training + task shaping + Transfer Package | Brain fog d=−2.9; 80% returned to work |
| Vanova 2026 (UCL) | 10 weekly 1hr telehealth sessions, goal-oriented strategies | Goal attainment d=1.57 at 3 months; telehealth-delivered |

Neither trial measured PEM. This protocol adapts both for someone actively pacing.

## Before You Start

### Step 1 — Pick Your 3 Goals

Choose 3 things that matter to you and are currently limited by brain fog. Be specific.

Examples:
- "Write thesis notes for 30 minutes without losing my train of thought"
- "Read a research paper and summarize the key points afterward"
- "Hold a 1-hour video call without needing a crash recovery nap"
- "Cook a meal that requires following multiple steps"
- "Stay focused on a book or show for 45 minutes"

*Write your goals here:*
1. _______________________________________________
2. _______________________________________________
3. _______________________________________________

### Step 2 — Baseline Rating

For each goal, rate how well you can do it *right now* on a 1–10 scale:
- 1 = can't do it at all
- 5 = can do it but it's a significant struggle
- 10 = doing it as well as before LC

### Phase 2 — Core Program (Weeks 2–10)

#### Component A: Speed of Processing Training (BrainHQ)
*Based on the computerized training used in Uswatte 2025*

- **What:** "Double Decision" and similar speed-of-processing games in BrainHQ
- **How much:** 20–30 minutes per session
- **When:** Green days only (🟢🟢 Second Green or better)
- **PacePoints cost:** Counts as *4–6 PacePoints* (cognitive strain = real strain)
- **Never on:** Red days, Yellow days, or First Green days

**Weekly target:**
- 🟢🟢 Second Green: 1 session if feeling stable
- 🟢🟢🟢 Third+ Green: Up to 2 sessions

#### Component B: Goal Task Shaping
*Based on the IADL shaping approach in Uswatte 2025*

Pick one of your 3 goals each week. Break it into smaller steps at your current capacity and practice starting there.

**How shaping works:**
- If your goal is "write thesis notes for 30 min" and that's currently impossible → start with 5 minutes
- Do 5 minutes consistently on green days for a week
- When 5 minutes feels easy → move to 8 minutes
- Never increase difficulty on a bad week
- Never attempt on red or yellow days

**Weekly focus:**
- Weeks 2–4: Goal 1
- Weeks 5–7: Goal 2
- Weeks 8–10: Goal 3
- (Rotate back to reinforce earlier goals as needed)

#### Component C: Daily Transfer Log
*Based on the Transfer Package in Uswatte 2025 + goal tracking in Vanova 2026*

After any cognitive work session, note briefly (2–3 sentences max):
- What I did: ___
- How it went: ___
- How I felt within 2 hours: ___
- HRV next morning: ___

This can live in the Cadence daily check-in as an optional field.

## Pacing Rules (Non-Negotiable)

These protect you from the PEM spiral that neither trial measured:

| Day Type | BrainHQ | Task Shaping | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🔴 Red (recovery <34) | ❌ | ❌ | Full rest |
| 🟡 Yellow (34–66) | ❌ | ❌ | No spend |
| 🟢 First Green | ❌ | ❌ | Rest day even if you feel good |
| 🟢🟢 Second Green | ✅ light (1 session) | ✅ short (start of shaping) | Watch HRV next morning |
| 🟢🟢🟢 Third+ Green | ✅ (up to 2 sessions) | ✅ | Can attempt fuller sessions |

**Stop rule:** If HRV drops >10 points the morning after a cognitive session, that session was too much. Back off one level.

## Check-ins & Evaluation

### Weekly (with Cleo)
- Review goal attainment ratings
- Review HRV pattern after cognitive sessions
- Adjust shaping level up or down based on data
- Flag any PEM signals

### Formal re-rating (Weeks 6 and 12)
- Re-score all 3 goals on the 1–10 scale
- Compare to baseline
- Decide whether to continue, adjust, or expand

---

## What Success Looks Like

**At 6 weeks:**
- Goals rated 2+ points higher than baseline = meaningful improvement (matches trial threshold)
- No increase in crash frequency
- HRV stable or trending up

**At 12 weeks:**
- Sustained improvement without needing to back off
- At least one goal feeling close to pre-LC capacity
- Able to increase shaping difficulty progressively

---

## Important Caveats

- This is adapted from research protocols, not a supervised clinical program. It is not medical treatment.
- The original trials excluded people with significant PEM — this protocol adapts for PEM but hasn't been tested in that context.
- If symptoms worsen consistently after sessions, stop and reassess. The goal is progress without cost.
- This addresses *functional compensation*, not the underlying biology. It works alongside (not instead of) medical treatment.
- Ideally reviewed with your PCP or a neurologist familiar with LC.
