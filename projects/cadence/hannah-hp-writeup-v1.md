## Assessment and Plan

### 1. Long COVID Syndrome / ME/CFS-phenotype

**Assessment:** This patient's presentation is most consistent with Long COVID with ME/CFS phenotype, characterized by: (1) post-infectious onset following SARS-CoV-2 + EBV co-infection, (2) severe PEM with 3-day lag demonstrated biometrically, (3) multisystem involvement, (4) autonomic dysregulation, and (5) near-complete failure to recover over 4 years. The leading mechanistic hypothesis, supported by emerging literature (Freire 2026, Iwasaki Lab), is persistent viral antigen/spike protein burden in gut and other tissues, driving ongoing immune dysregulation, HPA axis disruption, and microbiome perturbation. Alternative or co-contributing diagnoses include:
- **Persistent EBV reactivation:** VCA IgM was reactive June 2025, negative June 2026 — acute phase resolved; latent EBV with potential immunomodulatory effect persists. Valacyclovir protocol (Yale/Iwasaki) remains a pending discussion pending patient-submitted evidence.
- **ME/CFS (independent of COVID):** The severity and biometric pattern (PEM, 3-day lag, Ghali 2023 pacing adherence data) fulfills ME/CFS criteria per ICC/CCC. GET is contraindicated.
- **Small fiber neuropathy:** Less likely given normal formal ART, but patchy QSART forearm is of uncertain significance.
- **Mast cell activation syndrome (MCAS):** Tryptase 3.0 makes systemic mastocytosis unlikely; MCAS (without elevated tryptase) remains possible and may account for urticaria + multisystem symptoms. DO NOT MISS.

**Plan:**
- Continue pacing protocol (WHOOP-guided Repair Spectrum Framework). Current prescription: strain <2, PacePoints <6.
- Continue LDN 4.5 mg (helping pain).
- Discuss fluvoxamine per REVIVE-TOGETHER trial (Reis 2026, AIM) — FSS reduction −0.58 at day 90; benefits wane off-drug, likely requires maintenance dosing. Present to treating neuropsychiatrist.
- Follow up on valacyclovir paper — if Dr. Medley remains opposed, consider referring to ID/Virology for second opinion given EBV history.
- Consider referral for formal ME/CFS evaluation if not already complete.
- Patient education: reinforce that GET is contraindicated; heart rate monitoring at/below anaerobic threshold is the tool (PACELOC 2025 — 15% weekly PEM reduction with structured pacing).
- Follow up in 1–2 months; repeat WHOOP data review.
