## 1. Papulosquamous / Scaling Conditions

| Condition | Key Features | Distinguishing Clues | Workup | First-Line Tx |
|-----------|-------------|---------------------|--------|---------------|
| **Tinea corporis** | Annular, scaling border, central clearing | Active scaly edge, KOH+ hyphae, warm/moist body areas | KOH scraping | Topical terbinafine or clotrimazole × 2–4 wk |
| **Tinea cruris** | Groin/inner thigh, unilateral or bilateral | Spares scrotum (vs. candida), athletes/cyclists | KOH scraping | Topical antifungal |
| **Psoriasis** | Silvery-white adherent scale, erythematous plaques | Elbows, knees, scalp, nails pitting; Auspitz sign | Clinical ± biopsy | Topical steroids, calcipotriene |
| **Seborrheic dermatitis** | Greasy yellow scale | Scalp, nasolabial folds, ears, brows | Clinical | Zinc pyrithione, ketoconazole shampoo |
| **Pityriasis rosea** | Herald patch, Christmas tree distribution | Follows skin lines on trunk, self-limited 6–8 wk | Clinical | Reassurance, antihistamines |

## 2. Vesiculobullous Conditions

| Condition | Key Features | Distinguishing Clues | Workup | First-Line Tx |
|-----------|-------------|---------------------|--------|---------------|
| **HSV (sacral/buttock)** | Grouped vesicles on erythematous base | Unilateral, dermatomal (S2–S3), recurrent, pruritic or painful, PIH from healed lesions | Swab PCR or viral culture; HSV IgG serology | Valacyclovir |
| **Herpes zoster** | Dermatomal vesicular rash | Painful, unilateral, doesn't cross midline, older/immunocompromised | Clinical ± DFA/PCR | Valacyclovir within 72h |
| **Dyshidrotic eczema** | Deep-seated vesicles, hands/feet | Intensely pruritic, lateral fingers and palms | Clinical | Topical steroids, avoid triggers |
| **Bullous impetigo** | Flaccid bullae, honey crust | Children, staph, rapidly spreading | Clinical ± culture | Mupirocin or oral antibiotics |

## 3. Infectious Conditions

| Condition | Key Features | Distinguishing Clues | Workup | First-Line Tx |
|-----------|-------------|---------------------|--------|---------------|
| **Folliculitis (bacterial)** | Perifollicular papules/pustules | Buttocks, thighs, beard area; friction/occlusion history | Clinical ± culture | Topical mupirocin, benzoyl peroxide wash |
| **Cellulitis** | Diffuse erythema, warmth, swelling | Poorly demarcated, may have fever/leukocytosis | Clinical ± CBC | Antibiotics (cephalexin, TMP-SMX for MRSA concern) |
| **Candidiasis (cutaneous)** | Beefy red erythema with satellite pustules | Skin folds, moist areas; involves scrotum (vs. tinea) | KOH (budding yeast, pseudohyphae) | Topical nystatin or clotrimazole |
| **Impetigo** | Honey-crusted lesions | Children, face/extremities, contagious | Clinical ± culture | Mupirocin topical |

## 4. Inflammatory / Eczematous Conditions

| Condition | Key Features | Distinguishing Clues | Workup | First-Line Tx |
|-----------|-------------|---------------------|--------|---------------|
| **Atopic dermatitis** | Pruritic, excoriated, lichenified | Flexural creases, personal/family atopy history | Clinical | Topical steroids, moisturizers, avoid triggers |
| **Contact dermatitis (irritant)** | Erythema, scale, at exposure site | Corresponds to contact area; correlates with irritant exposure | Clinical ± patch testing | Remove trigger, topical steroids |
| **Contact dermatitis (allergic)** | Vesicular, intensely pruritic | Delayed (48–72h after exposure), corresponds to contact pattern | Patch testing | Remove allergen, topical steroids |
| **Lichen simplex chronicus** | Thickened, leathery plaque | Chronic itch-scratch cycle, single localized area | Clinical | Break itch-scratch cycle; topical steroids ± occlusion |
| **Nummular eczema** | Coin-shaped pruritic plaques | Well-defined, often on extremities, chronic | Clinical | Topical steroids, moisturizers |

## 5. Follicular / Occlusion Conditions

| Condition | Key Features | Distinguishing Clues | Workup | First-Line Tx |
|-----------|-------------|---------------------|--------|---------------|
| **Saddle sore / cycling folliculitis** | Buttock/perineal papules, pustules | Cyclists; friction from chamois, sweat, occlusion | Clinical | Hygiene, chamois cream review, antibacterial wash |
| **Hidradenitis suppurativa** | Nodules, abscesses, sinus tracts | Axilla, groin, inframammary; recurrent painful lesions | Clinical | Topical clindamycin, oral doxycycline, derm referral |
| **Keratosis pilaris** | Rough follicular papules | Upper arms, thighs, cheeks; asymptomatic or mild itch | Clinical | Moisturizers, lactic acid, urea cream |

## 6. Pigmentation Clues

| Finding | Suggests |
|---------|---------|
| Post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH) | Prior inflammation — supports recurrent/chronic process (HSV, tinea, eczema) |
| Hypopigmented patches | Tinea versicolor, vitiligo, post-inflammatory hypopigmentation |
| Violaceous/purple hue | Lichen planus, vasculitis |
| Silver/white scale | Psoriasis |
| Honey crust | Impetigo |
| Satellite lesions | Candidiasis |

## Cyclist-Specific Skin Conditions

Common in cyclists wearing padded shorts:

| Condition | Why Cyclists | Key Features |
|-----------|-------------|--------------|
| Tinea cruris/corporis | Warm, moist, occluded saddle zone | Scaling, annular, KOH+ |
| Folliculitis / saddle sore | Friction + sweat + bacteria | Perifollicular papules/pustules |
| Contact dermatitis | Chamois cream, synthetic materials | Corresponds to contact area |
| Friction intertrigo | Skin-on-skin or skin-on-chamois friction | Erythema, maceration |
