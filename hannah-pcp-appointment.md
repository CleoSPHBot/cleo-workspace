## EBV Reactivation & Valacyclovir — Clinical Rationale

### Why EBV matters in Long COVID
Dr. Akiko Iwasaki's lab at Yale (published in *Nature* and via the AMA) identified EBV reactivation as a key driver in a subset of Long COVID patients. In their cross-sectional study of 215 participants, elevated EBV antibody responses were one of the distinguishing immune features of Long COVID vs. recovered patients.

Mechanism: acute COVID-19 disrupts immune homeostasis → latent EBV reactivates → ongoing inflammation, fatigue, episodic pain.

### My specific evidence
- **June 2025 labs:** EBV Early Antigen (EA) IgM — *reactive* (active reactivation, not just prior exposure)
- **September 2025 colonoscopy:** patchy healing ulcers consistent with EBV-driven mucosal injury
- **Symptoms:** fatigue, episodic body pain — consistent with EBV reactivation profile

### Why valacyclovir (not acyclovir)
Valacyclovir is the prodrug form of acyclovir with ~3× better oral bioavailability. Fewer pills, more effective systemic levels.

### Requested prescription
> **Valacyclovir 1g TID (three times daily) × 3–6 months**

This dosing follows the Iwasaki Lab / Yale approach for EBV reactivation in Long COVID. Clinical trials of antivirals targeting EBV in LC are actively being developed (PolyBio Research Foundation, 2024).
