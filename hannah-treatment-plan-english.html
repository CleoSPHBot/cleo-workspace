About Your Treatments

BPC-157 / TB-500 / KPV / Larazotide

This combination targets tissue repair, gut healing, and inflammation control.

  • BPC-157 + TB-500: Regenerate tissues, reduce inflammation, improve energy and physical recovery. Also support gut health and mental clarity.
  • KPV: A peptide derived from a natural hormone (α-MSH). Powerfully anti-inflammatory and immune-modulating. Especially useful for gut and autoimmune-related inflammation.
  • Larazotide: Blocks "leaky gut" by protecting the tight junctions in the intestinal lining. Reduces toxins and inflammatory triggers crossing into the bloodstream. Helps microbiome balance and nutrient absorption.

MOTS-C

  • Optimizes mitochondrial function (cellular energy production)
  • Improves insulin sensitivity and fat metabolism
  • Reduces chronic fatigue
  • Crosses the blood-brain barrier — supports cognitive function
  • Anti-inflammatory and cardiovascular protective

NAD Nasal Spray

  • Boosts cellular energy (ATP production in mitochondria)
  • Supports DNA repair and cellular resilience
  • Improves mental clarity, focus, and reduces brain fog
  • Improves metabolism and insulin sensitivity
  • Supports detoxification

SEMAX + SELANK

Two neuropeptide nasal sprays used together for brain and nervous system support.

  • SEMAX: Improves focus, memory, and cognitive function. Neuroprotective.
  • SELANK: Reduces anxiety and nervous system hyperactivation. Calming without sedation.
  • Used in the morning/midday only — can be stimulating if taken too late.

DSIP (Delta Sleep-Inducing Peptide) Nasal Spray

  • Promotes deep (NREM) sleep — increases delta wave activity
  • Helps you fall asleep faster and wake up less during the night
  • Reduces stress and anxiety by modulating GABA and cortisol
  • Restores healthy sleep-wake cycle
  • Supports growth hormone release during sleep
  • Neuroprotective (antioxidant + anti-inflammatory)
  • Not sedating or habit-forming. Preserves REM sleep.

SS-31

A mitochondria-targeted antioxidant peptide. Protects mitochondrial membranes, reduces oxidative stress inside cells, and improves energy output — particularly relevant for fatigue-related conditions.

Thymosin Alpha-1

An immune-modulating peptide that helps regulate and strengthen immune function. Used in chronic infections, post-viral syndromes, and autoimmune conditions. Helps the immune system respond more appropriately — not overreacting or underreacting.

Armour Thyroid

A natural desiccated thyroid hormone containing both T3 and T4 (unlike synthetic levothyroxine which only has T4). Used when labs suggest thyroid function may be suboptimal — supports energy, metabolism, and mood.

DHEA

A hormone produced by the adrenal glands, often low in chronic illness. Supports energy, mood, libido, hormonal balance, bone health, immune modulation, and memory/focus.

Ketotifen

An antihistamine that also stabilizes mast cells — cells that, when overactivated, cause widespread inflammation, histamine reactions, and hypersensitivity. Particularly useful in Long COVID and MCAS (Mast Cell Activation Syndrome). Starting low (1 mg) and building up is standard to allow your body to adjust.
